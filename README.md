# ♟ Chess Game

## About the Game
This is a simple Chess Game where two players compete against each other on an 8×8 chessboard. The objective is to checkmate your opponent's king while protecting your own pieces.

## Gameplay Instructions

1. The game is played by two players: White and Black.
2. White always makes the first move.
3. Players take turns moving one piece at a time.
4. Each chess piece follows its own movement rules.
5. Capture your opponent's pieces by moving onto their occupied squares.
6. Protect your king from being captured.
7. The game ends when a player's king is checkmated or when the game results in a draw.

## Chess Pieces and Their Movements

- **King (♔):** Moves one square in any direction.
- **Queen (♕):** Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
- **Rook (♖):** Moves horizontally or vertically.
- **Bishop (♗):** Moves diagonally.
- **Knight (♘):** Moves in an L-shape.
- **Pawn (♙):** Moves forward one square and captures diagonally.

## Special Moves

- **Castling:** A special move involving the king and rook.
- **En Passant:** A special pawn capture.
- **Pawn Promotion:** A pawn reaching the opposite end of the board can be promoted, usually to a queen.

## Game Rules

- A player cannot make a move that leaves their own king in check.
- Check means the king is under attack.
- Checkmate means the king is under attack with no legal way to escape.
- Stalemate occurs when a player has no legal moves but is not in check.

## Objective

The main objective is to checkmate your opponent's king and win the game. Plan your moves carefully, protect your pieces, and outsmart your opponent!

**Good luck and enjoy the game! ♟**
